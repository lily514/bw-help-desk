## [hours:weather] Weather & Emergency Closings (updated 2026-08-01)
- Closes only when conditions are severe and staff travel is unsafe.
- Closings before opening are announced via the emergency notification system, family email, and the center's Facebook page. Keep your email and cell number current.
- If the center must close during the day, parents are notified and asked to pick up. In a building emergency (e.g., fire), children are moved to the Community Center across the street and parents are asked to pick up immediately.
- Fees are not adjusted for weather closings of two days or less.

## [fees:enrollment] Registration Fee & Deposit (updated 2026-08-01)
- One-time, non-refundable $40 registration fee (goes toward classroom supplies). Not charged again for continuously enrolled full-time children.
- To hold a spot: non-refundable deposit of one week's tuition + the $40 fee. The deposit is credited to the last week of care if the center gets two weeks' written notice of withdrawal.

## [fees:late_pickup] Late Pickup (updated 2026-08-01)
- Pickup deadline is 6:00pm.
- First late pickup: $10 minimum plus $1 per minute beyond the first 10 minutes. Second offense: $20 plus $1 per minute.
- Late fees may be waived at the Director's discretion for emergencies.
- Repeated lateness can result in withdrawal from the program.
- If a child is still at the center 30+ minutes after 6:00pm with no word from the family, police and/or Child Protective Services will be notified. Calling ahead about an emergency prevents this.

## [fees:vacation] Vacation Weeks (updated 2026-08-01)
- Up to two vacation weeks per year at half tuition, which holds the child's spot.
- Eligibility: first week after 6 months of enrollment; second week after 1 year. Vacation year starts on the enrollment date.
- Vacation fees are paid before the absence. Other absences are charged full rate.

## [fees:electives] Elective Activities (updated 2026-08-01)
- Optional karate, soccer, and gymnastics are offered on-site by outside vendors, billed by the vendor (not by Maple Grove).

## [enroll:process] Enrollment, Waitlist & Tours (updated 2026-08-01)

## [ratios:staff] Class Ratios & Staff (updated 2026-08-01)
- Max children per teacher: Infants 4 · Toddlers 5 · Twos 5 · Threes 10 · Fours 12 · Fives 15 · Six and up 15.

## [health:illness] Illness & Exclusion (updated 2026-08-01)
- Sick children cannot attend; families must arrange alternate care.
- A child will be kept home or sent home for: fever of 101°F or higher, diarrhea, vomiting, unexplained rash, or being unable to take part in normal activities (list is not exhaustive).
- Staff call parents if a child becomes sick or injured during the day, so phone numbers must always be current.
- Please call the center if your child will be absent due to illness. Communicable diseases must be reported; classroom families are told about possible exposure (not who). The center follows the Communicable Disease Chart posted in the lobby (copy on request).
- Children go outside daily unless weather or a doctor's note prevents it.

## [health:medication] Medication (updated 2026-08-01)
- In Indiana, anything applied to skin or taken by mouth counts as medication, including sunscreen, lotion, and diaper cream.
- Prescription meds: labeled for the child, in the original container.
- Over-the-counter meds (including sunscreen/diaper cream): given only with a written doctor's order; label the container with the child's name.
- Oral medication requires a parent permission slip each day unless directed by the prescription.
- Exceptions (e.g., nebulizer treatments) require Director approval.

## [health:toilet] Toilet Training (updated 2026-08-01)
- Children must be toilet trained before moving to the Threes room (special-needs exceptions apply). Staff help all Twos with toilet training.
- Elastic-waist pants are recommended for potty-training children.

## [health:infant_sleep] Infant Safe Sleep (updated 2026-08-01)
- Infants always sleep on their backs. Staff may not place infants on their side or stomach.
- Blankets, sleep sacks, and swaddles are not allowed (state regulation).

## [pickup:signin] Drop-off, Pickup & Authorized Adults (updated 2026-08-01)
- An adult must sign the child in and out every day (time + name of the person picking up).
- Don't leave until your child's teacher knows your child has arrived.

## [daily:supplies] What to Bring (updated 2026-08-01)
- Every child: one box of tissues and one box of wipes per month; at least one weather-appropriate change of clothes.
- Children in diapers: diapers and wipes in unopened packages, labeled with the child's name. Staff will notify you when supplies run low; backup diapers are kept for emergencies.
- Tennis shoes preferred; sandals discouraged.
- Don't send toys, money, candy, food, or gum. A favorite cuddly toy for naptime is welcome. No toy weapons.

## [daily:adjusting] Helping Your Child Adjust (updated 2026-08-01)
- Talk positively about the center beforehand and do an extended visit before the first day.
- Keep goodbyes short and confident; lingering tends to make adjustment harder.
- Keep a consistent drop-off and pickup routine, and be on time.

## [program:curriculum] Curriculum & Approach (updated 2026-08-01)
- Play-based, child-centered, learning centers in every room; whole-language approach to early literacy.
- Includes school-readiness activities, weekly hands-on themes, small-group time, art/music/dramatic play, daily-living skills, and annual developmental assessments.
- Parent-teacher conferences and assessments in June each year; conferences can be requested any time.
- At least 60 minutes of physical activity daily; children are not sedentary more than 60 minutes at a time except naps. Active play is never withheld as discipline.

## [program:screen_time] Screen Time (updated 2026-08-01)
- Ages 2 and under: no screen time.
- Ages 3–Pre-K: max 30 minutes per week; educational, ad-free, G-rated, tied to the lesson; videos at most once a month. An alternate activity is always offered. Never used as a reward.

## [program:discipline] Behavior Guidance (updated 2026-08-01)
- Positive reinforcement, clear age-appropriate expectations, and redirection.
- Time-outs are rare: max one minute per year of age, always explained.
- Never used: withholding food or basic needs, humiliation, threats, bribes, isolation, punishment for toileting accidents, or corporal punishment (grounds for immediate staff termination).
- Ongoing behavior concerns are handled privately with the family and may include a referral for outside support. If unresolved, the family may be asked to make other care arrangements.

## [program:special_needs] Special Needs & Inclusion (updated 2026-08-01)
- Children with special needs are welcomed under the ADA. Accommodations are planned with the family and health providers; staff can join IFSP/IEP meetings with parental permission.
- Each child's written care plan is followed, including in emergencies, and reviewed at least yearly. Information is kept confidential.

## [program:breastfeeding] Breastfeeding Support (updated 2026-08-01)
- A private, non-bathroom space with an outlet and chair is available for nursing or pumping; nursing in shared spaces is also welcome.
- Expressed milk can be stored in the center fridge in containers labeled with name and date.
- Formula and solids are given only if the parent requests them.

## [policy:visits_concerns] Open Door & Concerns (updated 2026-08-01)
- Parents may visit their child any time during the day.
- Share concerns with the teacher or Director; private meetings with the Director are scheduled within 24 hours when possible. Families are told in person or in writing how a concern is addressed.

## [policy:safety] Safety & Reporting (updated 2026-08-01)
- Staff are trained to recognize abuse and neglect and are legally required to report suspicions. Indiana child abuse hotline: 1-800-800-5556.
- Drug, alcohol, and firearm-free facility. Only pet: classroom fish; other animals need Director approval.
- Family information is confidential. Staff may not use family info for personal gain; the center discourages private babysitting arrangements between staff and families.
